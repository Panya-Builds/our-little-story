Dear Samruddhi,

Bala, hyachya aadhi mi tujhya sobat kay pan chukich vaglo asel, tya sathi sorry bala. Kadhi kadhi majhyakadun kahi goshti chuklya astil, pan majha intention kadhi hi tula hurt karaycha navhta.

Honestly, words are never enough to explain what you mean to me. Tu majhya life madhla ek khup special part ahes, ani tujhya sobat ghalavlela pratyek little moment majhyasathi khup precious aahe.

Aplya ya little memories, apli conversations, aple silly moments aani tujhya sobat haslele te small-small moments — he sagle mi manapasun cherish karto.

Bala, mala fakt evdhach sangaych aahe ki tu jashi ahes na, tashich kayam raha. Tujhi smile, tujhya sobatcha comfort ani tuji presence majhyasathi khup special aahe.

Life pudhe kuthhe gheun jail he aplyala mahit nahi, pan aapan ekemekanchi sath kadhich sodayachi nahi, kayam sobat rahayach aahe babdya. Aapan I love you so much ranuu majhya bachaaa.

And no matter how busy life gets, I hope these little memories always make you smile.

Thank you for being a beautiful part of my life, bala. You are, and will always be, someone very special to me. ❤️

Always yours,
Pranav ❤️
